**Tipsy Theoryy: Premium Liquor Delivery in Kenya**

Experience the ultimate convenience in liquor shopping with Tipsy Theoryy. Whether you’re hosting a party, celebrating a milestone, or enjoying a quiet evening at home, we bring your favourite spirits, wines, beers, and mixers directly to your door—fast, safe, and cold.

* **Massive Selection**: Browse a wide range of premium global brands and local favourites from licensed merchants in your area.

* **Loyalty Rewards**: Earn Tipsy Points with every order and redeem them for discounts on your future purchases.

**How it Works:**

1. **Browse**: Enter your location and find licensed stores in your delivery zone.
2. **Order**: Select your drinks and checkout in seconds.
3. **Pay**: Confirm the M-PESA prompt on your phone instantly.
4. **Track**: Watch your rider navigate to you in real-time.
